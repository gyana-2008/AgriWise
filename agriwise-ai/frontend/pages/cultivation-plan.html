Pioneer P3396 Hybrid (108-Day Cycle)

Current Stage: Day 25 — Early Vegetative (V4 Leaf)

DAY 0 • LAND PREPARATION & BASAL SOIL AMENDMENT
Deep Tillage, Laser Leveling & Basal Fertilizer Placement

Perform primary tillage with disc plough followed by two cultivator passes. Incorporate 5 tonnes well-decomposed FYM/vermicompost. Apply Basal DAP (5 bags total) and MOP (3 bags total) placed 3-5 cm below seed row to ensure deep root nourishment.

✓ Completed Basal DAP Placed
DAY 1 – 7 • SEED TREATMENT & SOWING
Precision Seed Drilling & Emergence Hydration

Treat Pioneer P3396 seed with Trichoderma viride (4g/kg) or certified fungicide. Drill seed with tractor seed-cum-fertilizer drill at 60 cm row-to-row and 20 cm plant-to-plant spacing (seed rate: 8 kg/acre). Give light emergence irrigation within 24 hours.

✓ Completed Plant Population: 28,000/acre
DAY 8 – 20 • GERMINATION & GAP FILLING
Seedling Emergence Verification & Early Weed Control

Check for uniform germination (expected >90%). Perform manual gap filling within 10 days of sowing. Apply pre-emergence herbicide (Atrazine 50% WP @ 800g/acre) within 48 hours of sowing in moist soil to suppress broadleaf weeds.

✓ Completed 94% Emergence Recorded
DAY 20 – 35 • CURRENT STAGE: EARLY VEGETATIVE (V4)
Action Required This Week
First Split Top-Dressing Urea Application & Inter-cultivation

Apply first split top-dressing of Neem Coated Urea (5 bags across 5 acres = 1 bag/acre) during calm weather when soil has good moisture. Run tractor cultivator or wheel hoe between rows to aerate soil and eliminate emerging weeds.

DAY 35 – 60 • KNEE-HIGH STAGE & FALL ARMYWORM MONITORING
Rapid Stem Elongation & Pest Scouting

Plants enter rapid exponential growth. Scout whorls twice weekly for Spodoptera frugiperda (Fall Armyworm). If pin-hole leaf damage exceeds 5%, apply recommended botanical neem formulations or university-approved biocides. Ensure soil moisture is non-limiting.

DAY 60 – 85 • TASSELING, SILKING & SECOND UREA SPLIT
Critical Flowering Phase & Second Nitrogen Dose

Most critical water sensitivity period! Any moisture stress during silking causes severe pollination failure and barren cobs. Maintain scheduled subsurface drip irrigation. Broadcast second split of Neem Coated Urea (1 bag/acre) at pre-tasseling stage.

DAY 85 – 108 • GRAIN FILLING & HARVEST PREPARATION
Black Layer Maturity Confirmation & Direct Mandi Dispatch

Grain attains physiological maturity when the black layer appears at the base of the kernel. Husk leaves turn dry and papery. Harvest mechanically with combine harvester or manual de-husking when grain moisture drops below 14-15% for maximum price realization at Khanna mills.
