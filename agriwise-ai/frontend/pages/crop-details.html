📖 Crop Description & Market Context

Maize is one of India's most dynamic commercial and industrial cereals. Beyond rural food use, strong macroeconomic demand from poultry feed compounding, bio-ethanol distilleries, and wet-milling starch factories in Khanna, Ludhiana, and Karnal has created an acute structural supply deficit (+41.8% regional shortage), maintaining spot mandi prices well above the government Minimum Support Price (MSP).
